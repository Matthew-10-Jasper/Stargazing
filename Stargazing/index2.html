Stargazing


Proxima Centauri

  1. Nearest star to the Sun, it is about 4.24 light-years away from Earth.
  2. A red dwarf star, it is much smaller, cooler, and dimmer than the Sun.
  3. Only 12% of the Sun's mass, despite being a star, it is relatively tiny.
  4. Cannot be seen with the naked eye.
  5. It frequently produces powerful stellar flares and radiation bursts.
  6. Its light takes 4.24 years to reach Earth.
  7. It is estimated to be about 4.8 billion years old, similar in age to the Sun.

Eagle Nebula

  1. The Eagle Nebula (M16) is a spectacular star-forming region located in the constellation Serpens.
  2. It is approximately 5,700–7,000 light-years away from Earth and spans about 70 light-years across.
  3. The nebula is best known for the Pillars of Creation, massive columns of gas and dust where new stars are born.
  4. It contains thousands of young stars that illuminate the surrounding clouds with intense radiation.
  5. The Eagle Nebula is composed mainly of hydrogen gas, helium, and cosmic dust, the essential materials for star formation.
  6. It was famously photographed by the Hubble Space Telescope in 1995 and later observed in greater detail by the James Webb Space Telescope.
  7. The Eagle Nebula is one of the most important astronomical objects for studying the birth and evolution of stars.
